What {{APP_NAME}} keeps

Signing in only proves which account you are. {{APP_NAME}} asks for no permission to post or read anything, and throws the sign-in tokens away at once. For each request you endorse, {{APP_NAME}} keeps a code computed from your account and that request with a key only the site holds, plus whether the account was under 30 days old. That's kept until the request is decided, and never more than about 13 months. To limit abuse, {{APP_NAME}} also keeps counters of recent endorsing for about a day, under a code for your account that changes daily and a code for your network. {{APP_NAME}} doesn't keep your handle, posts or followers. Counts are public; who endorsed isn't, though the site's operator, who holds the key, could check whether a particular account endorsed. Your sign-in lasts until you close the browser, and at most a day. Your Bluesky server will record that you signed in to {{APP_NAME}}, not what you endorsed. To count each account once, {{APP_NAME}} looks up your account's public records (its handle, server and creation date) when you sign in.
