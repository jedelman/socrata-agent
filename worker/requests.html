Endorse a request

If you want one of these added too, endorse it with your Bluesky account. Endorsements help the steward choose what to add next; they aren't votes. Asking {{APP_NAME}} still needs an invite (join the waitlist).

Endorsements are public. Endorsing saves a small record in your own Bluesky account that says you endorsed this request, with the date. Anyone can see it, and this page lists every account that endorsed, with its handle. If you say something here, your handle is attached to it.

{{APP_NAME}} asks your Bluesky server for one permission: to create and delete these endorsement records. It can't post, follow, or change anything else. Your browser talks to your server directly; {{APP_NAME}}'s server never sees your sign-in. {{APP_NAME}} keeps no record of who endorsed. The counts come from reading the public network.

To withdraw, press Endorsed (undo), or "Remove all my endorsements". That deletes the records from your account. Services that copied them while they were public may keep their copy for a while.

Accounts that call themselves bots, that Bluesky's moderators have labelled, or that are under 30 days old are flagged in the list. They still count.
