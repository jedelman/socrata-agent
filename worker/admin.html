Steward review: source requests

Accepting a request puts it on the public list and, at the next sync, on GitHub. A note you add is shown publicly. Private notes (sent before requests were public) are shown here only.

Bluesky endorsements are counted per account, not per person. Young accounts (under 30 days) and accounts of unknown age (did:web) are left out of the public number by default, and shown here split out. This page can't show which accounts endorsed; whoever holds INVITE_SECRET could compute it. A decision freezes the count into the request and deletes the per-account rows.
