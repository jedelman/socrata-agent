Steward review: shared sources

Sources people shared from their private answers with "Share these sources": titles and links only, never who shared them, the question or the answer. They publish at once, with no review first, and other people's answers start from them. Removing one takes it off the public list at once, and out of earlier research from then on, wherever it was found (shared sources, public answers, someone's own conversations); answers already given keep what they showed. Sharing it again, with or without a trailing slash, won't bring it back. Restoring puts it back.
