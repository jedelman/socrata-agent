What's on my block, and who decided it?

Ask about an address, or about anything in the city's open data. Every answer lists the queries behind it, the data to download, and who to contact.

Answers say what the city's records returned, not what is true on the ground: "I found" means the query found it. Check anything that matters against the trace and the original records.

Open source: github.com/jedelman/socrata-agent. Developers can run it locally with their own model key, or use it as a Claude Code skill.
